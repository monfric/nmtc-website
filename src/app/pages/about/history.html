Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to a Leading Medical College

{{ institution.name }} is a faith based non-profit medical training institution under the Catholic Archdiocese of Kisumu. It is situated approximately 60 kilometers from Kisumu City at Nyabondo Plateau, Nyakach Sub-County, Kisumu County, Kenya.

The institution was established in {{ institution.founded }} to provide training for midwives whose services were highly needed in the healthcare sector. It was recognized by the Nursing Council of Kenya as one of the nursing training institutions in 1960, offering training in Enrolled Community Nursing at Certificate level.

In 2001, the College expanded its academic programs by incorporating Diploma in Pharmaceutical Technology, which was recognized and registered by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology and the Pharmacy and Poisons Board. In 2004, the institution was upgraded to offer Diploma in Kenya Registered Community Health Nursing (KRCHN) at Basic level.

In 2015, the College further diversified its academic portfolio by introducing Diploma in Clinical Medicine and Surgery, which was recognized and registered by the Clinical Officers Council and the Ministry of Education. The first cohort graduated in the seventh graduation ceremony.

In 2018, the College introduced Diploma and Certificate programs in Health Records and Information Technology, registered by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. In 2026, the institution expanded its training programs further by introducing the Faculty of Health Service Support Assistant, with the first cohort reporting in March 2026.

Today, St. Joseph's Nyabondo Medical Training College continues to expand its contribution to healthcare education through diverse medical training programs. The institution remains committed to addressing professional skills gaps in the health sector by equipping learners with the knowledge, practical skills and professional attitudes required to improve healthcare service delivery across Kenya.
